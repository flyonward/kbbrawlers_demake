The host’s difficulty is used. Punctuation is ignored, spaces and capitals are not (wrong capitals deal half damage). Each hit chips an enemy’s HP and gives it a new phrase. Esc clears the box. Icons: 🌫 hides all words, ⚡ charges a big attack (land 4 hits to interrupt it), 💚 heals, ❄️ freezes foes, 🛡 shields the party when defeated. The boss fires attacks you can blunt by typing the green ward phrases.
